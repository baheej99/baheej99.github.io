About Me

I am an experienced IT Specialist, Senior Network Engineer, Cybersecurity Analyst, and University Lecturer with six years of experience and a strong background in designing, implementing, securing, and managing modern IT and network infrastructures. My expertise spans enterprise networking, cybersecurity, network administration, IT infrastructure, system troubleshooting, and technical education.

Throughout my professional career, I have worked with a wide range of networking and security technologies, including Cisco routing and switching, firewalls, VLANs, DHCP, NAT, VPNs, wireless networks, network monitoring, network security, and infrastructure management. I am passionate about building reliable, secure, scalable, and high-performance networks while proactively identifying and resolving technical challenges.

As a Cybersecurity Analyst, I have a strong interest in network security, threat identification, vulnerability assessment, security monitoring, incident analysis, and implementing effective security controls to protect organizational systems and data.

Alongside my technical career, I am also a University Lecturer and networking instructor, where I share practical and theoretical knowledge with students and aspiring IT professionals. My teaching areas include Computer Networking, CCNA, CCNP concepts, CompTIA A+, Networking Fundamentals, Cybersecurity, and IT technologies. I believe in combining academic knowledge with real-world practical experience to prepare students for professional careers in technology.

I am continuously developing my knowledge and skills in network engineering, cybersecurity, cloud technologies, systems administration, and emerging technologies, with a strong commitment to professional growth, innovation, and lifelong learning.

My mission is to design secure and reliable technology solutions, solve complex IT challenges, and empower the next generation of technology professionals through practical knowledge and education.
